# Taxonomy

The following top-level categories are used in `data/resources.yml` and the
README. Sub-topics are grouped for coherence rather than exhaustive
enumeration.

- **foundations** – Core federal technology policy, standards, and reference
  materials
- **rmf-80053** – NIST Risk Management Framework and SP 800-53 family
- **fedramp-oscal-ato** – FedRAMP, OSCAL, ATO automation, SSP/POA&M tooling
- **continuous-monitoring** – Assessment, continuous monitoring, and related
  automation
- **supply-chain** – SBOM, software assurance, SCA, SAST/DAST, secrets scanning,
  container/infrastructure scanning
- **devsecops** – Pipelines, policy-as-code, infrastructure-as-code, hardening
- **zero-trust-iam** – Zero trust architecture, identity and access management
- **cloud** – AWS GovCloud, Azure Government, Google Public Sector, related
  guidance
- **containers-k8s** – Kubernetes, containers, serverless platforms in
  government contexts
- **observability-ir** – Logging, observability, incident response
- **accessibility** – Section 508, WCAG, USWDS, accessible documents, video,
  procurement
- **ai-governance** – AI risk management, red teaming, model evaluation,
  security, privacy
- **privacy-records** – Privacy, records management, data governance
- **acquisition** – Templates, market research, statements of work, COR
  resources, vendor oversight, small-business guidance
- **oss-policy** – Open-source policy and federal guidance on OSS use
- **case-studies** – Public-sector implementations and lessons learned
- **training-communities** – Training materials and communities of practice
- **reference-architectures** – Reference architectures and patterns

Resources may belong to multiple categories.
